Where Wingman stands

A directory (Aerodromes, ATOs, DFEs, Weather, My minimums), no documents

About 90 links: 23 doc links, 10 study books, 29 UK Safety Sense, about 30 phone contacts, 19 webcam or video embeds

Flat coloured button lists per category, cover cards for Safety Sense. No search, no filters, no update dates

Legality rules

Link to SACAA, ATNS and SAWS, don't copy

Official sources stay current, and linking avoids hosting stale amendments.

FAA handbooks can be hosted or cached

US government works are public domain. Note that they are US rules.

ICAO docs: link to the ICAO store onlyRisk

pilotapp hosts Doc 8168 and 8162 copies on Adobe. Don't copy that.

UK and NZ CAA material: link only

Free to read but Crown copyright, so don't rehost it.

No leaked or recalled exam papersRule

Link only to paid providers, and add a note that practice banks are unofficial. There are no official SACAA practice papers to share.

How the best apps organise it

ForeFlight — Catalog drawer with Drives by country or provider, plus your own Binders

Tile or list view, nested folders, auto-update when a new version is out, one app-wide search, bookmarks and notes.

Garmin Pilot — Folders you can colour and reorder, with a built-in Garmin library

Bookmarks, highlights, sync, cloud imports.

SkyDemon — Basic user documents you can link to airfields

Says it is not a document manager.

pilotapp — Category button lists

No search and no version info.

Design for Wingman

Shelves

Official SA, Planning and NOTAMs, Study books, Air law, Logbooks, Exam prep, Contacts. Each is a glass box with the same expand motion as ATO and DFE.

One search box at the top, plus filter chips

Chips for Free, Official, PDF, SA only. Reuse the ATO and DFE filter panel and the slim search bar.

Premium cards

Source badge (SACAA, FAA, ICAO), a Free or Paid tag, the last date checked, and a little arrow for outside links.

Book shelf for study books

Cover tiles in the four theme tints rather than AI images, with a Continue reading row.

Saved and offline

Star items into My binder (stored on the device), and cache the public-domain FAA PDFs for offline use.

Fresh badge

The status page checks the AIC and AIP pages and shows New next to a shelf when something changes.

Emergency card pinned at the top

ARCC and SAR cancel numbers, tap to call.

Build plan

  1. Official SA shelf + search

    AIC, AIP and amendments, charts, CARs, licensing, exams, fees, medical examiners, accident reports. Links only.

  2. Study books shelf

    FAA PHAK, AFH, IFH, IPH, AIM, Weather and more, plus UK Safety Sense and NZ guides.

  3. Planning and NOTAMs plus Emergency contacts

    File2Fly, CAMU, Aviation Weather SA, ATNS numbers.

  4. Logbooks and Exam prep

    SACAA logbook rules explained, the four logbook apps and five exam providers, with Paid tags and an unofficial-source note.

  5. Saved binder, offline FAA PDFs, Fresh badges

    The premium layer.

Wingman Logbook (digital) coming later

A separate future build, not part of this Library page.

Accounts

Each pilot signs in so the logbook belongs to them and syncs across devices.

Secure storage

Flight records stored encrypted and backed up, never public.

SACAA 90-day print rule

SACAA accepts an electronic logbook if it is printed every 90 days (CAR 61.01.8), in the SACAA layout (CAR 61.01.7). Wingman would export a ready-to-print SACAA PDF and remind you when 90 days are up.
